Exponents
6 min

Python can also perform exponentiation. In written math, we might see an
exponent
Preview: Docs Loading link description
as a superscript number:

210210

However, typing superscript numbers isn’t always easy on modern keyboards. Since this operation is so related to multiplication, Python uses the notation **.

# 2 to the 10th power, or 1024
print(2 ** 10)

# 8 squared, or 64
print(8 ** 2)

# 9 * 9 * 9, 9 cubed, or 729
print(9 ** 3)

# We can even perform fractional exponents
# 4 to the half power, or 2
print(4 ** 0.5)
